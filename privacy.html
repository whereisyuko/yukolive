PRIVACY INFORMATION

YUKO LIVE — last updated 7 November 2026.

This notice explains how YUKO LIVE uses the information you submit when joining the live events list or completing the optional fan survey.

WHO TO CONTACT

YUKO LIVE is the music project responsible for this signup list. For privacy questions, to unsubscribe or to request access to or deletion of your details, email contact.yukocore@gmail.com.

WHAT WE COLLECT

When you sign up, we collect the city or town you enter, your email address and/or phone number, the notification options you choose, and the date and a unique ID for your signup.

If you choose to complete the separate Google Forms fan survey, we also receive the answers you submit there. Please do not include sensitive personal information in free-text answers.

HOW WE USE IT

  • To understand which places have interest in Yuko live shows and help plan events.
  • To send Yuko event announcements and related updates by email if you tick the email consent box.
  • To send Yuko event announcements and related updates by text if you tick the SMS consent box.
  • To manage the list, record consent and respond to privacy requests.

We rely on your consent for optional email and SMS marketing messages. You can withdraw that consent at any time. We use information needed to manage requests and maintain accurate records for our legitimate interests and applicable legal obligations, where relevant.

WHERE YOUR INFORMATION GOES

Signup information is submitted through Google Apps Script and stored in a Google Sheet accessible to the YUKO LIVE organiser. The optional fan survey is hosted using Google Forms. Google processes information when these services are used, potentially including processing outside the UK under its applicable safeguards. You can read Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your signup details. If email or SMS sending services are introduced, their providers may need to process the contact details required to deliver the messages; this notice should be updated to identify them before those services are used.

HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

We keep signup information while you remain subscribed and it is needed to plan or communicate about Yuko events. We review the list at least once a year and remove information that is no longer needed, subject to any legal need to retain limited records. If you withdraw consent or request deletion, we will remove your contact details from the active list unless we have a lawful reason to keep particular information.

YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

You can ask us to stop contacting you, withdraw your consent, request a copy of your personal data, correct inaccurate information, or ask for deletion. Depending on the circumstances, you may also have rights to object, restrict processing or request data portability. Contact contact.yukocore@gmail.com and tell us which email address or phone number you used to sign up so we can find your record.

You can also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

CHANGES TO THIS NOTICE

We may update this page if the website, messaging services or data practices change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
